Chapter three · Our method

We study it as ifin a laboratory

We come from research. Every choice in the apiary has data behind it, and we publish the data.

01

Stationary beekeeping

The hives stay in the same place all season: a single place for each honey.

02

Cold extraction

Only capped combs, at room temperature. Light filtering, no heating, no pasteurisation.

03

Monitored hives

Weight, temperature and humidity recorded every day and published. We harvest only when the bees' stores are safe.

04

Every batch analysed

A pollen analysis documents the botanical origin of each honey.
