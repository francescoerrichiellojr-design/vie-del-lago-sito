We don't run automated mailing lists. Any messages are sent manually and only at the user's explicit request.

03

Cookie Policy

The site uses technical cookies and local storage, needed for the pages to work and to remember your cookie choice: no consent is required for these (Art. 122, Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003). Google Analytics 4 analytics cookies are only installed if you click “Accept” in the banner; you can change your choice at any time from the link , also found in the footer of every page.

Our fonts are hosted on our own site, with no requests to external servers. The Google Maps map on the Contact page is only loaded if you click “Load the map”: in that case Google receives your IP address and may set its own cookies. Likewise, the map on The lake page only loads OpenStreetMap tiles if you click “Load the map”: in that case the OpenStreetMap servers receive your IP address; no cookies are set.
