Termsof sale

How an order from Vie del Lago works: from your message to delivery, with your rights as a consumer.

01

Who is selling

The products are sold by Vie del Lago Apicoltura, based at Via Contrada Pescara, 74, 83040 Conza della Campania (AV), Italy, VAT no. 10819081216, an agricultural producer selling honey from its own farm directly.

For any question: info@viedellagoapicoltura.it or WhatsApp from the Contact.

02

How an order is made

You can order in two ways: by adding jars to your order on the website and sending us the summary on WhatsApp or by email, or by writing to us directly. The summary is not a payment.

Within 24 hours on weekdays we confirm availability and the total and, if you choose delivery, its cost. The contract is concluded when you confirm the order in reply to our message. If a honey is not available we tell you beforehand.

03

Prices

The prices shown on the website are final prices, per 500 g jar or per set. Collection at our farm is free. The cost of any delivery depends on the town and we tell you before you confirm the order.

04

Payment

You pay on collection or delivery, by bank transfer, Satispay or cash. Nothing is paid on the website.

05

Collection and delivery

We don't ship by courier. You can collect your order at our farm, Via Contrada Pescara 74, 83040 Conza della Campania (AV), on the agreed day and time. Alternatively we deliver it ourselves to an address in Campania, on an agreed day.

06

Damaged jars

Check the jars on collection or delivery. If a jar is damaged or not intact, tell us straight away, or within 48 hours at the latest with a photo: we replace it or refund you, whichever you prefer.

07

Right of withdrawal

If you buy as a consumer you have the right to withdraw from the contract within 14 days of collection or delivery, without giving any reason, by writing to info@viedellagoapicoltura.it or on WhatsApp.

Honey is a sealed food product: withdrawal does not apply to jars opened after collection or delivery, because for hygiene reasons they cannot be returned (art. 59, letter e, of the Italian Consumer Code). Unopened jars must be returned within 14 days of notifying us of the withdrawal, by bringing them to our farm or agreeing with us how to collect them. We refund you within 14 days, using the same payment method as the purchase, once we have received them.

08

Guarantee

Consumers are covered by the legal guarantee of conformity under the Italian Consumer Code. If a jar does not match its description, write to us: we will replace it or refund you.

09

Food product information

Each honey's page lists the main information; the same details are on the label.

  • Product name: honey (wildflower, acacia, sulla)
  • Ingredients: honey, nothing added
  • Net weight: 500 g
  • Country of origin: Italy (Conza della Campania, AV)
  • Best before: shown on the jar, usually 2 years from production
  • Storage: in a cool, dry place, away from heat and direct light; not in the fridge
  • Food business operator: Vie del Lago Apicoltura, Via Contrada Pescara, 74, 83040 Conza della Campania (AV), Italy

Honey should not be given to children under 12 months. Crystallisation is a natural process and is not a defect.

10

Personal data

The details you give us for your order are used only to process and deliver it. Full details are in the Privacy & Cookie Policy.

11

Complaints and applicable law

For any complaint, write to us at info@viedellagoapicoltura.it: we reply within a few days and always look for a direct solution. The contract is governed by Italian law; for consumers, the courts of their place of residence have jurisdiction.

12

Updates

These terms may be updated. The terms in force on the day you confirm your order apply.
