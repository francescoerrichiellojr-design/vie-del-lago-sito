How to dispose of the packaging

Lid
Steel (FE 40). It goes in metal recycling, which in many Italian towns is collected together with plastic or glass.
Jar
Glass (GL 70). It goes in glass recycling.
Label
Paper (PAP 22). If it peels off easily it goes with paper, otherwise leave it on the jar.

Before you throw them away, empty and rinse the jar and unscrew the lid. Collection rules change from town to town, so check the ones where you live.
